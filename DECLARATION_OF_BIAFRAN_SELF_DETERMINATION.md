# DECLARATION OF THE BIAFRAN PEOPLE’S RIGHT TO
# SELF-DETERMINATION AND PEACEFUL INDEPENDENCE and Sovernity

**Draft for Public Consultation and Democratic Ratification**

**Proposed date of adoption:** 13.08.2026  
**Place of adoption:** Nigeria
**Adopting body:** OBINexus MMUCO OSes, Biafra

---

## PREAMBLE

We, the undersigned representatives and citizens of the Biafran people,
conscious of our history, identity, languages, cultures, communities and
shared political aspirations;

Remembering all persons who suffered or died during the Nigeria–Biafra
conflict and all subsequent periods of political violence, displacement,
discrimination and insecurity;

Affirming the equal dignity of every human being and the right of peoples
to determine their political status and pursue their economic, social and
cultural development;

Recognising that no individual, organisation, political movement or armed
group may permanently substitute its authority for the freely expressed
will of the people;

Rejecting hatred, ethnic supremacy, religious persecution, collective
punishment, terrorism and violence against civilians;

Seeking a future founded upon democracy, constitutional government,
public protection, accountable institutions and peaceful relations with
all neighbouring peoples;

Do hereby adopt this Declaration of the Biafran People’s Right to
Self-Determination and Peaceful Independence.

---

## ARTICLE I — THE RIGHT TO SELF-DETERMINATION

1. The Biafran people affirm their right to determine freely their
   political, constitutional, economic, social and cultural future.

2. We declare our common intention to constitute a sovereign,
   democratic and constitutional state to be known as the
   **Republic of Biafra**.

3. This intention shall be confirmed through a free, fair, transparent
   and independently observed democratic process.

4. No person shall claim that this Declaration binds every Biafran
   unless it has been ratified through a legitimate representative
   process or popular referendum.

---

## ARTICLE II — SOVEREIGNTY OF THE PEOPLE

1. All political authority belongs to the people.

2. Government shall exist only through their consent and shall remain
   accountable to them.

3. No president, monarch, military authority, political party, religious
   body, corporation or liberation organisation shall possess permanent
   authority above the Constitution.

4. Leadership shall be obtained and transferred through lawful,
   democratic and peaceful procedures.

---

## ARTICLE III — DEMOCRATIC RATIFICATION

1. A referendum shall be conducted among eligible persons within the
   affected communities under conditions that permit genuine political
   choice.

2. The referendum process shall include:

   - an independently maintained electoral register;
   - freedom to campaign for or against independence;
   - protection from intimidation and political violence;
   - independent domestic and international observers;
   - transparent counting and publication of results; and
   - a lawful procedure for challenging irregularities.

3. The precise referendum question, eligibility rules and territorial
   scope shall be determined through public consultation rather than
   imposed by any single organisation.

---

## ARTICLE IV — TERRITORY AND COMMUNITIES

1. The territorial boundaries of a proposed Republic of Biafra shall be
   determined through consultation with affected communities and
   peaceful negotiation with the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

2. No community shall be incorporated solely through force,
   intimidation or an unsupported historical claim.

3. No person shall be expelled, dispossessed or deprived of citizenship
   because of ethnicity, language, religion, place of origin or political
   opinion.

4. Legitimate disagreements concerning borders shall be resolved through
   negotiation, mediation, arbitration or another peaceful legal process.

---

## ARTICLE V — CONSTITUTIONAL GOVERNMENT

1. The Republic of Biafra shall be governed by a written Constitution
   adopted by the people.

2. The Constitution shall establish:

   - separation of legislative, executive and judicial powers;
   - an independent judiciary;
   - periodic and competitive elections;
   - transparent public finances;
   - civilian control of security institutions;
   - independent electoral and anti-corruption bodies;
   - local and regional self-government; and
   - enforceable limitations upon governmental power.

3. The constitutional order shall be guided by the following civic
   principles:

   - **OHA — Public Protection:** the state protects life and dignity
     before pursuing production or profit;
   - **IWU — Law and Policy:** law remains understandable, accountable
     and responsive to the people;
   - **IJI — Order, Governance and Equity:** public order exists to serve
     life, justice and equal participation.

4. Protection shall precede production, and order shall serve life.

---

## ARTICLE VI — FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS

1. Every person within Biafran jurisdiction shall possess equal rights
   and equal protection under the law.

2. The Constitution shall prohibit discrimination based upon ethnicity,
   race, colour, sex, language, religion, disability, neurodivergence,
   political opinion, national or social origin, property, birth or
   other status.

3. The rights of minorities, migrants, children, women, disabled and
   neurodivergent persons, and persons opposing independence shall be
   protected.

4. Every person shall enjoy freedom of conscience, religion, expression,
   association, peaceful assembly and political participation.

5. Torture, slavery, enforced disappearance, arbitrary detention,
   collective punishment and extrajudicial killing shall be prohibited.

6. The future Constitution shall recognise access to food, clean water,
   shelter, healthcare, education and basic energy as essential
   foundations of human dignity.

---

## ARTICLE VII — PEACE AND NON-VIOLENCE

1. This Declaration authorises no attack upon any civilian, community,
   institution or state.

2. No organisation may use this Declaration to justify terrorism,
   ethnic cleansing, kidnapping, political assassination, forced
   recruitment or destruction of civilian infrastructure.

3. Security organisations operating in the name of Biafra shall remain
   subject to civilian authority, constitutional law and international
   humanitarian standards.

4. Political disagreements shall be pursued through speech, voting,
   litigation, negotiation, peaceful assembly and democratic
   organisation.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — TRANSITIONAL PROCESS

1. Following democratic ratification, a temporary and representative
   transitional administration may be established.

2. Its authority shall be limited to:

   - negotiating transitional arrangements;
   - maintaining essential civilian services;
   - organising elections;
   - preparing a draft Constitution;
   - protecting civil and minority rights; and
   - transferring authority to an elected constitutional government.

3. The transitional administration shall publish its finances,
   decisions, negotiations and conflicts of interest.

4. It shall not convert temporary authority into permanent personal,
   military or one-party rule.

---

## ARTICLE IX — NEGOTIATION WITH NIGERIA

1. We invite the Federal Republic of Nigeria to enter peaceful,
   good-faith negotiations concerning the political future of the
   Biafran people.

2. Negotiations should address:

   - citizenship and residency;
   - borders and freedom of movement;
   - public assets and liabilities;
   - trade and transportation;
   - natural resources and environmental protection;
   - currency and financial arrangements;
   - pensions and public employment;
   - security and demobilisation;
   - protection of minority communities; and
   - continued family and cultural relationships.

3. We invite ECOWAS, the African Union, the United Nations and mutually
   accepted mediators to support a peaceful process.

4. Independence shall not be understood as hostility toward the peoples
   of Nigeria. Our objective is peaceful self-government, mutual dignity
   and regional cooperation.

---

## ARTICLE X — INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS

1. A democratically constituted Republic of Biafra shall seek peaceful
   relations with all states and peoples.

2. It shall respect international law, diplomatic agreements, human
   rights, peaceful commerce and the sovereign equality of states.

3. Following democratic ratification and the creation of accountable
   constitutional institutions, the Republic shall seek appropriate
   international recognition and membership of regional and
   international organisations.

---

## ARTICLE XI — PUBLIC ACCOUNTABILITY

1. Every organisation claiming to represent Biafra shall disclose:

   - its leadership;
   - its decision-making procedures;
   - its funding;
   - its membership or electoral mandate;
   - its policies concerning civilians and minorities; and
   - the means by which its leaders may be removed.

2. No organisation may claim exclusive representation merely through
   force, publicity, historical association or the declaration of its
   own leaders.

3. The liberation of a people must not produce a government that cannot
   itself be questioned.

---

## ARTICLE XII — ADOPTION AND SIGNATURE

This Declaration shall remain a proposal until adopted through a
representative and verifiable democratic process.

Upon ratification, the official record shall include:

- the referendum or assembly results;
- the applicable voting and eligibility rules;
- the names and mandates of authorised signatories;
- reports from independent observers;
- recorded objections or minority opinions; and
- the timetable for forming a constitutional government.

We make this Declaration not against another people, but in defence of
the right of every people to live with dignity, security, accountable
government and meaningful political consent.

We remember the dead.

We protect the living.

We commit ourselves to peace.

We submit our political future to the people.

**Signed:** Nnamdi Michael Okpala
